# Effective hyperparameters — reacher campaign, 2026-08-02

Companion to `RESULTS_PAPER_20260802.md`. Regenerate with
`scripts_20260729/dump_hypers.py` (reads the checkpoints, writes
`results/hypers_effective.json`).

**Provenance matters here and is marked throughout.**

- **[ckpt]** — recorded by the trainer *into the checkpoint it wrote*. This is
  ground truth: it is what the code actually saw, independent of any later edit
  to a driver script.
- **[driver]** — appears only in the committed driver, not in any artifact. The
  trainers do **not** serialise their optimiser settings, so these are
  declared-not-proven. They are reproducible from the scripts, but a silently
  ignored flag would not show up here.

Every value below was read back from the artifacts behind the reported numbers
(`lip4_leak6_*`, `pwmabl_*_s1000_terminal_*`, `window3_*_e005_g098`), not
transcribed from intent.

## 6. Gaps in this record

1. **Optimiser settings are [driver], not [ckpt].** Neither `train_lip_ac.py`
   nor `train_window.py` serialises lr / steps / expectile / γ / replay-prob
   into its output. They are reproducible from the committed scripts, but the
   artifacts cannot independently confirm them. PWM is the exception — it
   records `gamma` and `objective`, and both check out.
2. **The value's expectile and γ are not in the artifact**, only its
   architecture. Two values trained at different expectiles are
   indistinguishable from their checkpoints alone; they are distinguished here
   only by filename convention (`_e005_g098`).
3. **Env seeds are not recorded in any artifact** — they are eval-time arguments
   and live only in the driver and the per-cell log filenames.
