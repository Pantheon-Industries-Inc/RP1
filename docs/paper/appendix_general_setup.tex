\subsection{General Setup}
\label{app:results-general}

\paragraph{Data and evaluation protocol.}
All world-model encoders and dynamics predictors are frozen throughout; critics and
planners are trained purely from cached latents. Each domain provides $10{,}000$
episodes: value functions and planners train on episodes $0$--$7{,}999$, and all
evaluations draw start/goal states from the held-out episodes $8{,}000$--$9{,}999$.
Every choice -- including the stopping point of Table~\ref{tab:rp1-hypers-uniJ} -- is
made on the validation draws $\{48,49,50,51\}$, which are never reported. Reported
numbers use the disjoint draws $\{42,43,44\}$, $50$ episodes each, and are the
\emph{median} over six planner training seeds $\{0,\dots,5\}$ of each seed's mean over
those three draws; we give the mean over seeds alongside. Test draws are touched once
per cell, after the stopping point is fixed.

\paragraph{Planning protocol.}
All planners use $5$-step action chunks and optimize $H{=}5$ chunks ($25$ primitive
steps) open loop, replanning every $5$ chunks (receding horizon $5$). The goal is the
state $h$ primitive steps ahead and the episode budget is $2h$ steps; TwoRoom and Cube
evaluate $h\in\{25,100\}$ ($h25$, $h100$), Reacher $h{=}25$. Simulator evaluations run
under EGL with a pinned render device and at most one evaluation per GPU; we verified
that this parallelism is bit-identical to fully serialized evaluation before adopting it.

\paragraph{Objectives.}
Every planner scores the predicted terminal state with one of the two objectives of
Sec.~\ref{sec:baseline-hyperparameters}: the latent-distance objective
$C_{\mathrm{latent}}(\hat z_N,z_g)=\lVert\hat z_N-z_g\rVert_2^2$, or the value objective
$C_{\mathrm{value}}(\hat z_N,z_g)=V_\psi(\hat z_N,z_g)$, the goal-conditioned
temporal-distance critic of Sec.~\ref{sec:value-learning} (MRN quasimetric-style head)
trained on the frozen cached latents of each base.

\paragraph{One recipe for every domain.}
Unlike the per-domain configurations of earlier work, every number we report for RP1
comes from the single configuration of Table~\ref{tab:rp1-hypers-uniJ}: the same actor,
critic and refiner settings for all three environments and both world-model bases. Two
quantities vary, and neither is a tuned hyperparameter. The discount is set by the
horizon being planned, $\gamma{=}0.98$ for $h25$ and $\gamma{=}0.99$ for $h100$, so that
$1/(1-\gamma)$ covers the horizon; consequently each TwoRoom and Cube cell is two
independent stacks, while Reacher is $h25$ only. And the critic reads a $2$-frame latent
window on Reacher rather than $1$, because joint velocity is unobservable from a single
frame; this is a property of the observation, not a choice we searched over.

\paragraph{Stopping point.}
The only quantity selected per cell is \emph{when to stop}, and it is selected jointly
over the critic and the actor. The offline teacher is snapshotted every $3{,}000$ steps
to its $18{,}000$-step cap and the actor every $2{,}000$ steps to its $12{,}000$-step
cap; each of the $6\times6$ (teacher budget, actor step) pairs is trained and scored on
the validation draws, and \emph{one} pair is fixed for the whole cell by the mean score
over the six seeds, ties broken toward the smaller budget and then the earlier step. All
six seeds are then evaluated at that single pair. No per-seed selection is performed at
any point, and the same rule is applied to the learned baselines
(Sec.~\ref{sec:baselines}).
