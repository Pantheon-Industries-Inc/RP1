\paragraph{Data and evaluation protocol.}
All world-model encoders and dynamics predictors are frozen throughout;
critics and planners are trained purely from cached latents. Each domain
provides $10{,}000$ episodes: value functions and planners train on episodes
$0$--$7{,}999$, and all evaluations draw start/goal states from the held-out
episodes $8{,}000$--$9{,}999$. RP1 uses one configuration in every domain
and for both world models (Table~\ref{tab:rp1-hypers-all}); the only
within-run selection is the choice of training snapshot, made on the
disjoint draws $\{48,\ldots,51\}$, which are never reported. Reported
numbers are the median over six planner training seeds $\{0,\ldots,5\}$ of
the per-seed mean over the three predeclared evaluation draws
$\{42,43,44\}$ ($50$ episodes per draw and cell); the seed means differ from
the medians by at most $1.6$ points in every cell. Baseline planners have no
training seed and are averaged over the same three draws.

% Replaces "Specific setup." in app:results-reacher (draws now 42-44; the
% wider 42-47 protocol is gone unless re-run):
\paragraph{Specific setup (Reacher).}
Reacher follows the shared protocol (six training seeds, draws
$\{42,43,44\}$, $18$ evaluations per base and tolerance). Success is
first-hit: all joints within $\tau$ radians of the goal configuration,
scored in a separate simulator pass per $\tau$ with termination on success.
The RP1 critic reads the terminal state through a two-frame latent window
(lag $5$, standardized latents), the minimal window in which arrival
velocity is observable; the baselines read single-frame costs.

% ------------------------------------------------ limitations paragraph ----
\paragraph{One configuration, and what it costs.}
The numbers in Tables~\ref{tab:tworoom}--\ref{tab:dyna} come from a single
RP1 configuration shared across domains, bases and horizons; an earlier
draft tuned the actor per cell. The shared recipe matches or improves the
per-cell numbers in ten of twelve columns (Cube improves in every column, by
$2$--$14$ hard points), and gives up ground in TwoRoom on PLDM at $h100$
($85.3$ against $96.0$ per-cell), where the six seeds spread from $74.7$ to
$91.3$: on the less faithful world model the actor sometimes settles into a
basin of plans the model mispredicts, and the median seed pays for it. We
consider the loss the price of a configuration that was not chosen after
seeing the test columns, and report the per-cell numbers only as a
reference.
