\paragraph{Deep Model-Predictive Optimization (DMPO).}
DMPO keeps the MPPI update and learns a residual on it: a network reads the sampling
distribution and the $N$ rollout costs (no state, no gradient) and emits a gated mean
correction, a covariance update, and a learned warm-start shift \citep{sacks2024deep}.
We train it with the paper's objective, PPO, closed inside the frozen world model
against the same critic $V_\psi$ the RLP row deploys, for the paper's budget of $1000$
policy-improvement iterations ($4$ closed-loop decisions per imagined episode, batch
$16$). We deploy a budget of $256$ rollouts per decision ($256{\times}1$).

\paragraph{Learning-to-Optimize MPC (L2O-MPC).}
L2O-MPC, DMPO's predecessor, learns the \emph{whole} sampling update rather than a
residual: a network reads the mean, covariance, and $N$ costs and emits a gated
replacement mean \citep{sacks2022learning}. As it is not a working optimizer untrained,
it is trained by DAgger imitation of a larger-budget MPPI expert, computed through the
same frozen world model and critic. We match the paper's DAgger structure exactly --
$20$ rounds, $\beta_k = 0.8^k$, and an expert budget far above the learner's
($N{=}512$ against $M{=}64$) -- but not its literal optimizer budget of roughly $10^6$
gradient steps per seed: in the original setting a step is a small MLP update, whereas
here it is a world-model unroll. We instead train to convergence, $18{,}000$ steps, and
verify convergence per cell from the selection curve below. We deploy a budget of $256$
rollouts per decision ($64{\times}4$), matching DMPO.

\paragraph{Equal treatment.}
Both baselines are given the protocol we give our own method. Each uses the cell's
locked critic $V_\psi$, trained for exactly the teacher budget that cell's RLP row
selected, on the same world model, data split and evaluation draws. Each is trained at
three seeds, snapshotted periodically through training (DMPO every $200$ of its $1000$
iterations; L2O-MPC every $3{,}000$ of its $18{,}000$ steps), and \emph{one} shared
snapshot per cell is chosen by the mean score over seeds on the validation draws, ties
broken toward the earlier snapshot -- the identical rule, and the identical draws, by
which RLP selects its (teacher, actor) pair. Reporting a fixed final iterate instead
would not be the same treatment: DMPO's PPO return is a trendless walk over its budget,
and its final iterate is selected in only one of ten cells.
