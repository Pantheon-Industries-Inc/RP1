# L2O-MPC baseline results — 2026-08-15/16

L2O-MPC (Sacks & Boots, ICRA 2022, arXiv:2212.02603) trained and evaluated
per the port notes in [README_l2o.md](README_l2o.md):
DAgger imitation (beta = 0.8^k, 20 rounds) of an MPPI expert (N = 512) through
the frozen world model against the frozen `value_td` critic; learner M = 64
samples x K = 4 iterations = **256 forward unrolls per decision** (DMPO's
deployed budget). Three optimizer seeds per cell, report draws 42/43/44,
50 episodes each. Cube/Reacher held-out (episodes 8000–9999); TwoRoom on the
authors' full 10k pool (the shipped contract). Reacher: window-3 quasimetric
(e0.05, lag 5), first-hit at h25. amax matched per cell to the other planner
rows (cube 1.6/4.5, tworoom 2.5, reacher 2.2/1.8).

In-cell value-MPPI controls (same critic, 9,000 rollouts) were run for h25
draw 42 (Cube/TwoRoom) and draws 42/43 (Reacher) before being dropped for
wall-clock; the prior campaign's protocol-matched MPPI rows are quoted
alongside. Protocol caveat: Reacher's earlier tables report draws 42–47;
everything here is 42–44.

## Reacher window ablation (success %, tau = 0.1 / 0.05)

Identical recipe to the rows above with a **window-1** critic in place of the
window-3 one (single frame, e0.05); `amax` held at 2.2/1.8 so the critic
window is the only difference. Jobs 7342/7343, tags
`l2o-reacher-{lejepa,pldm}-w1-20260816`.

| planner | LeJEPA | PLDM |
|---|---|---|
| **L2O-MPC, window-1** | **90.9 ± 2.6 / 69.6 ± 2.2** | **89.3 ± 2.7 / 60.9 ± 1.7** |
| L2O-MPC, window-3 (above) | 93.3 ± 1.1 / 74.9 ± 2.3 | 94.7 ± 0.0 / 77.3 ± 1.4 |
| *window-1 penalty* | *−2.4 / −5.3* | *−5.4 / −16.4* |
| value-w1 MPPI (App C.3 replication) | 74.0 / 42.0 | 60.0 / 38.7 |
| value-w1 CEM (App C.3 replication) | 97.3 / 82.0 | 96.0 / 76.0 |

Two readings. (a) The window-1 penalty is **tolerance-dependent**: 2–5 points
at tau 0.1 but 5–16 at tau 0.05, the signature of a critic that cannot
represent arrival velocity — the same mechanism the RLP window ablation
found (~+9 for window-3 at the tight tolerance). PLDM is hit hardest, as it
is everywhere information is reduced. (b) L2O's **margin over its MPPI expert
widens** under the weaker critic (+27.6/+22.2 at tau 0.05, versus +8.9/+13.3
at window-3): DAgger fits the learned update to whatever cost it is handed,
so it degrades more gracefully than the fixed MPPI reduction. It still does
not reach CEM at either window. Both jobs trained cleanly (imitation loss
0.58 → 0.05 LeJEPA, 0.34 → 0.07 PLDM), so this is not an underfitting
artifact.

## Reading

1. **The paper's claim replicates where the search landscape is benign.**
   L2O-MPC matches or beats the 9,000-rollout MPPI it imitates at 35x fewer
   rollouts on TwoRoom h25 (+6/+9), TwoRoom LeJEPA h100 (**90.9 vs 64.0**,
   the headline cell), and all four Reacher cells (+7 to +13). The in-cell
   controls agree with the prior campaign's independent MPPI rows to ~1–3
   points everywhere, so the ruler is calibrated.
2. **It never overtakes CEM or RLP.** CEM (same 9,000-rollout budget as MPPI)
   stays ahead in every environment, and RLP dominates every cell at 9
   rollouts. Ordering across the board: RLP > CEM > L2O ≥ MPPI.
3. **Cube is the honest failure mode.** Parity with MPPI on LeWM (64.4 vs
   64.0), below it on PLDM (58.9 vs 68.0), both ~10 points over the no-op
   floor and below latent CEM. The DAgger imitation losses on cube rose as
   beta decayed (learner-state distribution), i.e. the update rule never fully
   fit cube's 125-dim plan space at paper hypers; the paper's own escalation
   (hidden 2048/4096) is the natural follow-up arm.
4. **TwoRoom PLDM h100 collapses (48.2) while LeJEPA holds (90.9)** — with
   value-CEM at 89.3 and RLP at 96.0 on the same base, the critic is not the
   limit; the PLDM base's long-horizon landscape defeats a 256-sample budget
   specifically (its latent-objective rows sit at 33–52 there too).
5. **Seed variance is tiny** (PLDM reacher literally 0.0) except cube LeWM —
   consistent with imitation of a deterministic expert, and very unlike RLP's
   actor-seed spread.
6. **A weaker critic hurts L2O less than it hurts MPPI** (window ablation
   above), which is the flip side of finding 3: the learned update adapts to
   the objective it is trained against, so it loses least where the *cost* is
   degraded and most where the *search* is hard (cube).

**Hyperparameters were not swept.** Every value is the paper's published
setting (Adam 1e-3, dropout 0.1, 20 DAgger rounds at beta = 0.8^k, Halton
samples, fixed diagonal covariance, expert N >> learner M); the two repo-side
knobs were inherited rather than tuned (`amax` from the matched RLP/DMPO cell,
64 x 4 = 256 rollouts to equal DMPO's deployed budget). That is deliberate for
a baseline — a swept L2O against unswept siblings would flatter it — but it
scopes finding 3 to "at paper hypers": the paper itself escalates hidden size
to 2048/4096 on harder tasks, and cube's rising DAgger loss is the symptom
that would motivate exactly that arm.
