# L2O-MPC baseline campaign — 2026-08-15/17

L2O-MPC (Sacks & Boots, *Learning to Optimize in Model Predictive Control*,
ICRA 2022, [arXiv:2212.02603](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.02603)) ported as
`model=l2o` / `core/solver=l2o`. Method, paper-component map, and the list of
deliberate deviations: [README_l2o.md](README_l2o.md).

Per-environment results:

- [**OGBench Cube**](RESULTS_L2O_ogbench.md) — accuracy + the wall-clock speed
  table for every planner
- [**TwoRoom**](RESULTS_L2O_tworoom.md) — includes the campaign's headline cell
- [**Reacher**](RESULTS_L2O_reacher.md) — window-3 rows plus the window-1
  ablation

## Shared protocol

DAgger imitation (`beta_k = 0.8^k` over 20 rounds) of an MPPI expert with
N = 512 samples, computed through the frozen world model against the frozen
`value_td` critic; the learner uses M = 64 samples × K = 4 iterations = **256
forward unrolls per decision**, matching DMPO's deployed budget so the two
learned-optimizer rows differ in method, not compute. Nothing is trained on
environment interaction: the expert is pure computation, and DAgger's state
distribution is the inner-loop iterate chain on offline cache windows.

Three optimizer seeds per cell, report draws 42/43/44, 50 episodes each. Cube
and Reacher are held out (train 0–7999, eval 8000–9999); TwoRoom follows the
shipped full-pool contract. `amax` is inherited per cell from the matched
RLP/DMPO configuration, never tuned.

In-cell value-MPPI controls (same critic, 9,000 rollouts) were run for h25
draw 42 on Cube/TwoRoom and draws 42/43 on Reacher, then dropped to save
wall-clock; the prior campaign's protocol-matched MPPI rows are quoted
alongside and agree with them to 1–3 points.

**Hyperparameters were not swept.** Every value is the paper's published
setting (Adam 1e-3, dropout 0.1, 20 DAgger rounds, Halton samples, fixed
diagonal covariance, expert N ≫ learner M); the two repo-side knobs (`amax`,
the 64×4 budget) were inherited rather than tuned. That is deliberate for a
baseline — a swept L2O against unswept siblings would flatter it — but it
scopes the Cube result to "at paper hyperparameters".

## Campaign-level findings

1. **The paper's claim replicates where the search landscape is benign.**
   L2O-MPC beats the 9,000-rollout MPPI it imitates at 256 rollouts on TwoRoom
   (h25 both bases; LeJEPA h100 **90.9 vs 64.0**) and in all four Reacher cells
   (+7 to +13).
2. **It never overtakes CEM or RLP.** Ordering across all three environments:
   RLP > CEM > L2O ≥ MPPI.
3. **Cube is the honest failure mode** — parity with MPPI on LeWM, below it on
   PLDM, with a DAgger loss that rose as beta decayed.
4. **TwoRoom PLDM h100 collapses (48.2) while LeJEPA holds (90.9)**, and
   value-CEM's 89.3 on the same base and critic shows the binding constraint is
   the 256-sample budget, not the objective.
5. **Seed variance is tiny** (Reacher PLDM tau=0.1: identical on all three
   seeds) — imitation of a deterministic expert, unlike RLP's actor-seed spread.
6. **A weaker critic hurts L2O less than it hurts MPPI** (Reacher window-1
   ablation): the learned update adapts to the cost it is trained against, so it
   loses least where the *objective* is degraded and most where the *search* is
   hard.
7. **Speed: RLP is fastest once both are graphed** (~30 ms vs 45.3 ms per
   decision at B=1), but L2O's 256 rollouts cost only ~1.5× RLP's 17 because
   wall-clock follows sequential depth, not rollout count. L2O's CUDA-graph
   capture is bit-exact (max_deviation 0.000e+00); DMPO's regresses. Details and
   caveats in the [OGBench page](RESULTS_L2O_ogbench.md#wall-clock-speed-lewm-1h200-fp32-whole-decision-mean-ms).
